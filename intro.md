## 🛠️ Featured Tools & Technologies

| Tool / Technology | Category | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **React 18 & TypeScript** | *Frontend Architecture* | Robust, type-safe framework powering responsive component state, telemetry interfaces, and interactive modal dialogs. |
| **Three.js & React Three Fiber (`@react-three/fiber`)** | *3D Graphics & WebGL* | Declarative WebGL renderer displaying real-time 3D space environments, lighting, camera choreography, and the central Space Shuttle stack. |
| **`@react-three/drei`** | *3D Utilities* | Provides advanced 3D helpers including `OrbitControls` for 360° model inspection, high-performance image textures, and spatial text rendering. |
| **NASA 3D Resources & Open Data** | *Scientific Assets* | Authentic NASA GLB models (Opportunity, Ingenuity, Apollo Lunar Module, InSight, Pioneer 10) and planetary exploration photography. |
| **Lenis (`@studio-freight/lenis`)** | *Scroll Experience* | Smooth inertial momentum scrolling engine that drives cinematic navigation through the helical orbital artifact corridor. |
| **Tailwind CSS** | *Styling & Aerospace UI* | High-contrast, dark-matter space aesthetic with tactical mission-control HUD layouts, glowing accents, and responsive typography. |
| **Lucide React** | *Iconography* | Crisp, aerospace-grade vector iconography for HUD telemetry, audio controls, timeline navigation, and status badges. |
| **HTML5 Audio & Web Speech API** | *Audio & Accessibility* | Dual-channel narration system streaming authentic voice recordings with time scrubbing, equalizers, and browser speech synthesis fallback. |
| **Vite** | *Build Tooling* | Ultra-fast next-generation frontend development server and bundler delivering instant Hot Module Replacement (HMR). |

## ⏱️ 45-Second Video Pitch / Narration Script

*Target Word Count:* **105 words**  
*Estimated Speaking Rate:* **140 words per minute (WPM)**  
*Total Runtime:* **45 seconds**

```text
[00:00 - 00:10] Hook & Mission
Welcome to Novochronos. Across the Moon and Mars lie humanity’s greatest robotic pioneers—abandoned, silent, but never forgotten.

[00:10 - 00:23] 3D Tech Stack & NASA Data
Built with React Three Fiber and official NASA 3D assets, Novochronos brings discarded space hardware back to life. Real-time WebGL renders high-fidelity rovers and landers inside an interactive orbital corridor.

[00:23 - 00:36] User Experience & Audio
Driven by Lenis smooth scrolling and Tailwind CSS, visitors can inspect authentic artifacts in three-sixty degrees, while listening to first-person archival audio narrations directly from the Martian and Lunar surface.

[00:36 - 00:45] Call to Action
Novochronos: preserving humanity’s cosmic footprint through interactive digital storytelling.
```

### Script Breakdown & Director Notes

* **[00:00 - 00:10] Hook:** Start on deep space void; camera slowly reveals the Space Shuttle launch stack and orbiting artifact cards. Tone is majestic and reflective.
* **[00:10 - 00:23] Tech & NASA Assets:** Cut to close-up 3D orbit around the Opportunity Rover and Apollo Lunar Module. Highlight real-time Three.js lighting and reflections.
* **[00:23 - 00:36] Interactive Inspection & Audio:** Click on an artifact card to open the 3D inspection podium. Demonstrate drag-to-rotate controls and trigger the narration audio player with waveform activity.
* **[00:36 - 00:45] Closing Outro:** Zoom out to show full orbital helix with mission control HUD overlay.
