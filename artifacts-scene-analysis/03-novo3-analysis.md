## 2. Scientific & Engineering Principles

1. **Lunar Module Staging & Thermal Architecture**:
   - The Lunar Module (LM-5 *Eagle*) comprised two distinct stages: the Ascent Stage and the Descent Stage.
   - The Descent Stage served as the launch platform for the Ascent Stage, utilizing explosive guillotine inter-stage fasteners and cable disconnects.
   - Construction utilized lightweight aluminum alloys covered in multi-layer insulation (MLI) blankets: aluminized Mylar, yellow-gold Kapton polyimide film, and black Pyromark ceramic-coated nickel-alloy panels on the plume-deflector surfaces.
2. **Space Weathering & Mechanical Regolith Preservation**:
   - Lunar vacuum ($10^{-12} \text{ torr}$) and the absence of atmospheric weathering (no liquid water, no wind) prevent erosion.
   - Anthropogenic traces—including Neil Armstrong's and Buzz Aldrin's Apollo Lunar Surface Extravehicular Activity (EVA) bootprints (tread pattern 31)—will persist for millions of years, altered only by slow micrometeoroid bombardment.
3. **Corner-Cube Retroreflection Optics**:
   - The Apollo 11 Laser Ranging Retroreflector (LRRR) consists of a pallet containing 100 fused silica corner-cube prisms ($3.8 \text{ cm}$ diameter each) recessed in an aluminum panel.
   - Optical principle: A corner cube consists of three mutually perpendicular reflecting surfaces. Any incident light ray undergoes total internal reflection three times and is reflected back along a vector strictly parallel to the incoming beam, regardless of the angle of arrival.
4. **Lunar Laser Ranging (LLR) & Celestial Mechanics**:
   - Terrestrial observatories (e.g., McDonald Observatory, Apache Point Observatory, Observatoire de la Côte d'Azur) transmit nanosecond pulses of frequency-doubled Nd:YAG laser light ($\lambda = 532 \text{ nm}$, green).
   - Round-trip photon transit time ($\approx 2.56 \text{ seconds}$) is timed with picosecond accuracy ($c = 299,792,458 \text{ m/s}$).
   - Key scientific discovery: The Moon's semi-major orbital axis expands outward at a rate of $38.3 \pm 0.9 \text{ mm/year}$ ($3.8 \text{ cm/year}$) caused by tidal friction dissipation in Earth's oceans transferring angular momentum to the lunar orbit.

## 4. Visual Realism & Rendering Directives

- **Vacuum Rendering Model**: Absolute absence of atmospheric scattering. Shadows are completely black ($0\% \text{ ambient light}$) except where illuminated by secondary regolith albedo and Earthshine.
- **Multilayer Insulation (MLI)**: Physically wrinkled gold Kapton and aluminized Mylar foils with realistic micro-creases, taped seams, and delicate flutter rigidity.
- **Corner Cube Shader**: Internal total internal reflection (TIR) shader with high index of refraction ($n = 1.46$) fused silica, rendering accurate optical retroreflection.
