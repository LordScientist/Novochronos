## 2. Scientific & Engineering Principles

1. **Micro-Rover Architecture & Extreme Mass Constraint**:
   - Total mass: 10.5 kg (23.1 lb) terrestrial mass ($\approx 4.0 \text{ kg}$ on Mars); dimensions: $65 \text{ cm long} \times 48 \text{ cm wide} \times 30 \text{ cm high}$ (comparable to a school backpack).
   - Powered by a 0.22 $\text{m}^2$ Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) solar panel producing ~16 W at noon and non-rechargeable Lithium-thionyl chloride ($LiSOCl_2$) primary D-cell batteries.
   - 8-bit Intel 80C85 processor running at 100 kHz with 512 kB RAM and 176 kB flash storage.
2. **First Extraterrestrial Rocker-Bogie Mobility**:
   - Inverted rocker-bogie passive suspension mechanism with 6 cleated aluminum wheels ($13 \text{ cm}$ diameter, $8 \text{ cm}$ width) driven by custom DC motors with planetary gearboxes.
   - Overcame obstacles 1.5 times the wheel diameter without active sensors or complex suspension damping.
3. **In-Situ Alpha Proton X-Ray Spectrometry (APXS)**:
   - Rear-mounted APXS sensor head utilizing Curium-244 ($^{244}Cm$) alpha radiation to excite characteristic X-ray emissions and backscattered alpha particles from rock targets.
   - Contact spectrometry on basaltic and andesitic rocks ("Barnacle Bill", "Yogi Rock", "Scooby Doo"): identified high silica concentrations ($SiO_2 \approx 55\%$) and differentiation processes consistent with crustal melting and volcanic recycling.
4. **Fluvial Geomorphology of Ares Vallis**:
   - Landing site selected in an ancient outflow channel where catastrophic mega-floods debouched into Chryse Planitia during the Hesperian era.
   - Identified imbricated, semi-rounded pebbles, fluting, and sedimentary scour marks confirming discharge rates exceeding millions of cubic meters per second of liquid water.
5. **Generational Legacy**:
   - Provided the definitive proof-of-concept that planetary rover operations could survive thermal night-cycles (using three radioisotope heater units containing 1 Watt of plutonium-238) and navigate autonomously via laser-stripe proximity sensors, establishing the engineering blueprint for Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance.
