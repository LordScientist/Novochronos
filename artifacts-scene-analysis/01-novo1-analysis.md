## 2. Scientific & Engineering Principles

1. **Planetary Ingress, Descent & Airbag Attenuation**:
   - Multi-lobed Vectran fabric airbags cushioned the initial 12-G impact and bounced ~28 times across Meridiani Planum before coming to rest in Eagle Crater.
   - Petal deployment mechanism with pyrotechnic cable cutters and retractor winches to expose the rover egress path.
2. **Rocker-Bogie Passive Articulation**:
   - Differential rocker mechanism maintaining equal weight distribution across six independently driven cleated aluminum wheels.
   - Capability to traverse obstacles larger than wheel diameter (25 cm) without tipping.
3. **Aqueous Mineralogy & In-Situ Geochemistry**:
   - Discovery of gray hematite ($Fe_2O_3$) spherules ("Martian blueberries") embedded in sulfate-rich sedimentary outcrop rocks.
   - Concretion mechanisms requiring standing, percolation, and evaporation of acidic groundwater in the Noachian/Hesperian boundary epoch.
   - Scientific payload utilized: Mössbauer Spectrometer, Alpha Particle X-Ray Spectrometer (APXS), and Microscopic Imager on the Instrument Deployment Device (IDD).
4. **Odometry, Slip Management & Long-Distance Traversals**:
   - Cumulative driving distance of 45.16 km (28.06 miles), exceeding an Olympic marathon (42.195 km).
   - Hazard avoidance software (GESTALT) and hazard cameras (Hazcams) navigating around ripple dunes (e.g., Purgatory Dune trap extraction in 2005).
5. **Atmospheric Optical Depth ($\tau$) and Solar Photovoltaic Extinction**:
   - Multi-junction Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) solar arrays generating ~600-900 Wh/sol under clear skies ($\tau \approx 0.5$).
   - The June 2018 planet-encircling dust event escalated atmospheric opacity to $\tau > 10.8$, reducing solar irradiance below the 22 Wh/sol survival threshold needed for clock operation and survival heaters.

## 4. Visual Realism & Rendering Directives

- **Regolith Shader**: Multi-layered micro-roughness shader combining coarse basalt sand grains, specular silica flecks, and fine cohesive iron oxide dust that cakes into wheel treads.
- **Solar Array Shading**: Multi-cell silicon/GaAs panels with deep navy iridescent anti-reflective coatings, layered with an alpha-masked dynamic procedural dust accumulation shader.
- **Physical Camera Language**: 35mm to 85mm anamorphic focal lengths with subtle lens breathing, realistic depth of field, and micro-camera vibration during dust storm sequences.
