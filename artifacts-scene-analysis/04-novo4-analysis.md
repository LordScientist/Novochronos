## 2. Scientific & Engineering Principles

1. **Ultra-Sensitive Planetary Seismology (SEIS)**:
   - Seismic Experiment for Interior Structure (SEIS) instrument developed by CNES/IPGP.
   - Six seismic sensors: three Very Broad Band (VBB) inverted pendulum oblique seismometers in an evacuated sphere and three Short Period (SP) high-frequency sensors.
   - Sensitivity: Capable of detecting ground displacements smaller than $10^{-12} \text{ meters}$ (less than the Bohr radius of a hydrogen atom, $0.53 \times 10^{-10} \text{ m}$).
2. **Aerodynamic & Thermal Isolation (WTS)**:
   - Wind and Thermal Shield (WTS): A distinctive gold-domed aerodynamic housing with chainmail thermal skirts placed over SEIS to shield the sensors from Martian thermal swings (over $80^\circ \text{C}$ daily fluctuation) and gust-induced ground tilts.
3. **Internal Structure of Mars & Planetary Ultrasound**:
   - Recorded over 1,319 marsquakes and multiple bolide/meteorite impact acoustic and shear events.
   - Inversion of P-wave (primary compressional) and S-wave (secondary shear) transit times, core-reflected phases ($PcP$, $ScS$), and core-transmitted phases ($PKP$).
   - Groundbreaking discovery: Mars possesses a crust 24 to 72 km thick, a lithospheric mantle $\approx 1,560 \text{ km}$ deep, and a large, completely liquid iron-nickel-sulfur molten core with a radius of $1,830 \pm 40 \text{ km}$.
4. **Photovoltaic Power Degradation & Dust Settlement Dynamics**:
   - Twin 2.15-meter diameter decagonal UltraFlex solar arrays generating ~4,500 Wh/sol at landing.
   - Atmospheric dust settling (airfall accumulation) without cleaning events (no dust devils passed over the panels) steadily reduced daily output from 4,500 Wh down to under 300 Wh.
   - Lack of mechanical wipers or active articulation motors necessitated progressive shutdown of scientific subsystems until terminal power loss on Sol 1,446 (December 2022).

## 4. Visual Realism & Rendering Directives

- **Gold Thermal Shielding**: High-reflectance gold polyimide foil with procedural wrinkling, micro-perforations, and edge-creased chainmail skirting.

- **Subsurface Seismic Visualizer**: Physically plausible wavefield propagation shader depicting spherical compressional ($P$) and transverse ($S$) wavefronts traveling through viscoelastic basalt and mantle rock.
